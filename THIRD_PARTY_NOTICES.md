# Third-party material

- The ICLR 2027 style and bundled LaTeX support files in `paper/` retain their original copyright/license notices. The official distribution is https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip.
- enwik8 is obtained separately from https://mattmahoney.net/dc/textdata.html. The repository contains reconstruction code and hashes; the corpus is excluded from Git and release archives.
- Pythia and its public variants are obtained from EleutherAI on Hugging Face. Their model cards and upstream licenses govern those weights. The repository publishes revision identifiers and checksums, not copies of public pretrained weights.
- Python dependencies are installed separately and retain their upstream licenses. The tested requirements are recorded in `experiments/environments/`.
